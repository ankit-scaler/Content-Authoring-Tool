## 2. Program 1: Scaler Academy (Software Development)

### 2.1 Program Level Rules & Governance
- `*` **Customized Module**: This module is customised at learner level i.e. a learner can opt for this module at any point during the course post the completion of Data Structures & Algorithms module, once they have demonstrated their command on the concepts of Data Structures & Algorithms module.
- `**` **Specialisation Rule**: Only 1 Specialisation is allowed per learner. Recorded lectures for the other can be availed via Student Dashboard. At Least 1 Specialisation is mandatory for successful completion of the course.
- `***` **Elective Rule**: A learner can do as many electives as they want, but only after completion of Core Curriculum.
